Money skills

Learn the basics

Short lessons that match what you can track in EduFin.

The 50/30/20 rule

Split what comes in: about 50% for needs (rent, food, transport, fees), 30% for wants and 20% for savings. Your dashboard's savings rate shows how close you are.

Pay yourself first

When your allowance or bursary arrives, move a fixed amount to savings straight away, before you spend. Small and regular beats big and rare.

Build an emergency fund

Aim for one month of essential costs set aside for surprises: a medical bill, a broken phone, a late payment. Then grow it to three months.

Budget the leaks

Airtime, data, snacks and transport add up fast. Put a monthly limit on them on the Budgets page and watch the bars.

Track every franc

Record transactions the day they happen. After a month you will see patterns you did not expect, and that is where the savings are.

Plan for fees early

Tuition and book costs come at known times. Divide them by the months left and save that amount each month so the term start is not a shock.
